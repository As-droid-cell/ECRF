## Part One · THE MEASUREMENT

The measurement is the 360°. Nothing else.
The zodiac, the sectors, the four elements, the three modes, the twenty-seven nakṣatras and the AstroGnosis entity roster are tools laid on the measurement. None of them is the measurement. Every division in this framework — 40 stations, 120 offsets, 1080 units, 12 modality divisions — divides the 360° of a plane, counted from that plane’s own zero.

### The Law of Distributed Coherence

Any method or tool is accepted so long as it can be applied to the measurements of the system. Diversity can coexist only where it abides by that one law. Admission is generous; the measurement does the discriminating.

The clause has teeth. It excludes anything with no direction to address, anything requiring a zero the system does not have, and anything demanding collapse to a single plane. A law that admits everything is not a structure.

## PART TWO · DEFINITIONS BY LAYER

Ordered so that reading from the top teaches the system. An alphabetical index follows in Part Six.

### 0 · The Measurement

- The measurement — The 360° of a plane. Nothing else. The zodiac, the sectors, the four elements, the three modes, the twenty-seven nakṣatras and the AstroGnosis entity roster are tools laid on the measurement. None of them is the measurement.
- Law of Distributed Coherence — The system's admission rule: any method or tool is accepted so long as it can be applied to the measurements of the system. Diversity can coexist only where it abides by that one law. Admission is generous; the measurement does the discriminating. The clause has teeth — it excludes anything with no direction to address, anything requiring a zero the system does not have, and anything demanding collapse to a single plane.
- ECRF — EMERGENT Celestial Reference Framework. The structural system: three sovereign planes plus the Depth Field, read by an observer.
- ECRF-4 — The four-structure form: three sovereign planes and the Depth Field.
- Structure vs Reading — Structure is geometry and does not move when an interpretation changes. Reading is what is laid over it. The locked declaration separates them so that changing a reading can never move a structure.
- Sovereign — A plane is sovereign when its readings are computed in its own frame from ICRS directly, never converted from another plane's values. Sovereignty is a computational property, not a claim of rank.

### 1 · Frames and Planes

- ICRS — International Celestial Reference System. The fixed, non-rotating coordinate frame all three planes are built from. The system's ground truth.
- Ecliptic — The plane of Earth's orbit. The most local of the three: it is the only face carrying zeros built from Earth's own furniture — the equinox and the lunar nodes.
- Galactic — The plane of the Milky Way's disk. IAU 1958 realisation in ICRS. Carries no equinox and does not precess.
- Supergalactic — The plane of the Local Supercluster's flattening. De Vaucouleurs' realisation. Carries no equinox and does not precess; its zero is admittedly conventional.
- Pole — The direction perpendicular to a plane. Each plane has a north and south pole, and each pole is itself a direction the framework can address.
- Wall — A sovereign plane, seen from the terrain side. An object at latitude 0 on a plane stands on that plane's wall.
- Node / crossing node — Where two sovereign planes intersect. Three pairs of planes give six crossing nodes, each an ascending and descending pair.
- Obliquity — In the physical layer, the angle between a body's rotation pole and its orbital pole. For Earth, 23.441487° at this epoch.
- ±P4 — The Xibalba axis. The direction equidistant from all three sovereign poles, at circumradius 41.8418467°. Derived from the pole set, not declared. Reads lattice 270.000000° on all three faces.
