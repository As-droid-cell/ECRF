# ECRF · Part 2 — Definitions by Layer

This file extracts and organizes the definitions from Part Two of the ECRF Foundations text.

0 · The Measurement (summary)
- The measurement: the 360° of a plane. The system's admission rule is the Law of Distributed Coherence.
- ECRF: EMERGENT Celestial Reference Framework — three sovereign planes plus the Depth Field (ECRF-4).
- Structure vs Reading: structure is geometric and invariant; readings may change.

1 · Frames and Planes
- ICRS: International Celestial Reference System — the fixed non-rotating frame used throughout.
- Ecliptic: Earth's orbital plane; carries equinox and lunar nodes; precesses.
- Galactic: Milky Way disk plane (IAU 1958 realization); does not precess.
- Supergalactic: Local Supercluster flattening (de Vaucouleurs); conventional zero; no precession.
- Pole, Wall, Node / crossing node, Obliquity, ±P4 — definitions and roles in the framework.

2 · Time and Epoch
- UT / UTC: stored datum; moments are recorded in UT.
- UT1: Earth's rotation time used for observer computations; UTC is its proxy within 0.9 s.
- TT: Terrestrial Time for ephemeris positions; derived from UTC via leap seconds.
- ΔT: TT − UTC and its epoch dependence.
- Epoch, J2000, "Of date", Precession, LST, Hour angle — operational definitions.

3 · Observers and Viewpoints
- Viewpoint: topocentric, geocentric, heliocentric, barycentric — each supported.
- Topocentric: default system datum under Ruling 04.
- Geocentric: reclassified as a smoothed observer; differences to topocentric noted.
- Heliocentric, Barycentric: use-cases and angular impacts described.
- Observer slab: ±8° band defining residence at a plane.
- Zenith and Horizon definitions in the ECRF context.

4 · Rings
- Ring: a plane-partition into named arcs; plane-bound and non-portable.
- Arc: one named segment; arcs are unequal and defined by actual boundaries.
- TruSky 13 (ecliptic arcs), TruSky 27 (galactic arcs), F/F/V ring (supergalactic arcs) explained.
- Degrees into an arc, Zero marks, Tropical vs Sidereal vs Draconic longitudes, Ayanāṃśa — definitions.

5 · Houses and Sectors (summary)
- Angles: ASC/DESC/MC/IC on each sovereign plane — the framework carries twelve angles.
- Pullen Sinusoidal Ratio: the locked house method; r parameter and example values for this chart.
- H / House, GQ / Gauquelin sectors described.
